Annual Report

LUNGLEI VENGLAI KOHHRAN PAVALAI PAWL CHANCHIN


Lalmanthanga,
KPP Secretary, 2025
A TOBUL LEH DIN DAN:
Kum 2004 khan Aizawl veng hrang hrangah Gospel Thunder Team-in rawngbawlna an nei a, Thlarau Thianghlimin hna a thawk a, kohhranho pawh an hlim hle a ni. Chutia Kohhran an hlim chhoh mek lai chuan Mizoram Presbyterian Kohhranin kan la hriat ngai loh ‘Pavalai Pawl’ chu July 21, 2014 khan Tuikhuahtlang Kohhran-ah din a lo ni ta a. Chumi hnu chuan ITI Veng, Saikhamakawn, Khatla, Upper Republic leh Venghnuai (an ni hi Pavalai ti lovin Team-in an indin) ah te Pavalai Pawl an ding leh a. Heng ding hmasa panga te hian dt. 17th February, 2015 zan khan Tuikhuahtlang Kohhran Hall-ah ‘Joint Fellowship’ hlimawm tak an hmang nghe nghe a ni. Hemi hi kangkai zelin Mizoram hmun hrang hrangah Pavalai Pawl a ding thar zel a. Hetiang a lo nih takah chuan Mizoram Synod chuan mumal zawk leh rualkhai zawka Pavalai Pawl a kal theih nan inkaihhruaina te siamin, a hmingah ‘Kohhran Pavalai Pawl’ a vuah a, enkawltu atan Synod Revival Committee a ruat fel ta bawk a ni. Hetianga Kohhran Pavalai Pawl Synod-in a hriatpuia, inkaihhruaina hial a siam takah chuan Lunglei Venglai Presbyterian Kohhran Committee pawhin him tawkah ngaiin KPP din ve zai a rel ta a, kum 2013’ Kum Tawp Committee’ dt. 13.12.2013 a neih chuan Lunglei Venglai Kohhran Pavalai Pawl a din ve ta a ni. KPP din ni atan pawh 13.12.2013 hi a pawm fel nghal. Tichuan, Office Bearers Post 5 a siam nghala, chungte chu :– Chairman, Vice Chairman, Secretary, Asst. Secretary leh Treasurer. Tichuan, kum 2014 chhunga hruaitu (OB) tur pawh a ruat nghal a (chunmgte chu a hnuaiah tarlan a ni). KPP hruaitu (OB) te hian Committee member 20 aia tam lo an ruat ang tia thu dahin, KPP stationary lei nan leh bul tan nan Rs. 1000/- a pe bawk (Lunglei Venglai KPP hi 2013 December thlaa din a nih avangin hemi kum hian Committee member ruat bak chu hmalakna dang neih hman a ni lo (kum 2014 atangin hmalak tan a ni). Tichuan, kum 2017 atang khan Kohhran Committee remtihnain OB Post pakhat ‘Finance Secretary’ Post siam belh a ni a, 2017 kum thleng hian Kohhran Pavalai Pawl Chairman leh Secretary te hi Kohhran Committee-in a ruat thin a, OB Post dang zawng hi KPP member ten kan thlang thin. Amaherawhchu, kum 2018 atang khan Chairman leh Secretary te hi Kohhran Committee-in ruat tawh lovin OB Post zawng zawng hi member kal khawmten kan thlang zel tawh a ni.
